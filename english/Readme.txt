This folder contains my mind maps for structuring core English grammar.

The goal is to show an approach to organizing knowledge and to share ready-to-use cheat sheets. The maps focus on structure, relationships, and quick navigation. Each file covers a specific domain.

Information was collected and systematized from multiple sources. Some simplifications are intentional. For accuracy, consult your teacher or reliable references.
